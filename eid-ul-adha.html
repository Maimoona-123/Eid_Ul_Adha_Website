The Sacred Story
قصہ حضرت ابراہیم علیہ السلام

The Story of Ibrahim (AS)

A timeless tale of unshakeable faith and divine love — the story that gave birth to one of Islam's most sacred celebrations.

The Blessing
The Gift of Ismail (AS)
رَبِّ هَبْ لِي مِنَ الصَّالِحِينَ

After decades of heartfelt prayer, Ibrahim (AS) was blessed with his son Ismail (AS) through his wife Hajirah. This child was the answer to years of longing — a gift so precious that Ibrahim's love for him was immeasurable. Ismail (AS) grew into a virtuous and beloved young boy by his father's side.

"My Lord, grant me from among the righteous." — Ibrahim's prayer (Quran 37:100)
1
The Divine Command
The Dream of Sacrifice
إِنِّي أَرَىٰ فِي الْمَنَامِ أَنِّي أَذْبَحُكَ فَانظُرْ مَاذَا تَرَىٰ

Allah tested Ibrahim (AS) through a recurring dream — a vision that he must sacrifice his beloved son Ismail. The dreams of Prophets are divine revelation. Ibrahim (AS), shaken yet faithful, approached Ismail honestly: "O my son, I have seen in a dream that I must sacrifice you. So see what you think." This was the greatest trial any father could face.

"O my son, I have seen in a dream that I [must] sacrifice you. So see what you think." — Quran 37:102
2
The Son's Faith
Ismail's Perfect Submission
قَالَ يَا أَبَتِ افْعَلْ مَا تُؤْمَرُ سَتَجِدُنِي إِن شَاءَ اللَّهُ مِنَ الصَّابِرِينَ

Ismail (AS) — still a young boy — responded with extraordinary spiritual maturity. Rather than fear, he said with complete surrender: "O my father, do what you are commanded. You will find me, if Allah wills, among the patient." He even asked his father to blindfold himself so the sight of his son would not weaken his resolve. Both father and son stood firm in their faith.

"Do what you are commanded. You will find me, if Allah wills, among the steadfast." — Ismail (Quran 37:102)
3
Shaytan's Deception
Iblis Tries to Stop Them
فَرَجَمُوهُ بِسَبْعِ حَصَيَاتٍ

On the way to Mina, Shaytan appeared to Ibrahim (AS), Hajirah, and Ismail (AS) — trying to tempt them away from Allah's command. Each time, they rejected him firmly, throwing stones at him in defiance. This act is commemorated every Hajj through the ritual of Rami — the symbolic stoning of Shaytan at the three Jamarat pillars in Mina.

Stoning Shaytan — remembered every Hajj at the three pillars of Mina
4
The Supreme Moment
The Knife That Would Not Cut
فَلَمَّا أَسْلَمَا وَتَلَّهُ لِلْجَبِينِ

Ibrahim (AS) laid his son down and placed the knife to his throat — blindfolded so sorrow would not weaken him. But by Allah's will, the knife could not cut. Both father and son had fulfilled their submission completely. Allah saw their sincerity and tested them no further. They had truly surrendered — the meaning of "Islam."

"And when they had both submitted and he put him down upon his forehead…" — Quran 37:103
5
Divine Mercy & Reward
The Ram from Jannah
وَفَدَيْنَاهُ بِذِبْحٍ عَظِيمٍ

In that supreme moment, Allah called out: "O Ibrahim! You have fulfilled the vision!" Jibril (AS) descended with a white ram from Jannah — and Ismail was spared. Allah rewarded their faith with boundless mercy. This moment is the origin of Qurbani — we sacrifice an animal every Eid to honor this act and draw closer to Allah through obedience and devotion.

"And We ransomed him with a great sacrifice." — Quran 37:107
6
The Essence

Significance of Eid ul Adha

A sacred occasion of devotion, sacrifice, and gratitude rooted in one of Islam's greatest stories.

Ibrahim's Trial

The ultimate test of faith — a father commanded to sacrifice what he loved most. Ibrahim (AS) passed, and his legacy lives on every Eid ul Adha.

01
Divine Mercy

Allah replaced Ismail with a ram — a testament to His infinite mercy for those who submit completely to His will with sincerity.

02
Sharing & Unity

Qurbani meat divided in three — for family, relatives, and the poor — ensuring every Muslim shares in Eid's joy and blessings.

03
Hajj Connection

Eid ul Adha coincides with the climax of Hajj — millions completing the fifth pillar of Islam in the holy land of Makkah.

04
How We Celebrate

Sacred Traditions

Beautiful practices that unite Muslims across the world on this blessed occasion.

Eid Prayer

Two rak'at congregational prayer in the morning — collective worship and joy.

Qurbani

Ritual slaughter of a halal animal in Allah's name — Sunnah of Ibrahim (AS).

Eidi

Elders shower children with gifts and money — spreading joy across generations.

New Clothes

Wearing new attire symbolizes renewal and celebrating Allah's blessings.

Family Feasts

Visiting relatives and sharing traditional dishes — strengthening bonds of kinship.

Charity

Distributing meat and Sadaqah to the poor — ensuring all share in Eid's joy.

Supplications

Step by Step

Qurbani Guide

Understanding the sacred rite of sacrifice — performed in remembrance of Ibrahim (AS).

1
Perform Eid Prayer First

Qurbani must be performed after Eid prayer. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever slaughters before the prayer should slaughter again."

Essential
2
Choose a Valid Animal

Goat/sheep (1 person), cow/camel (up to 7 people). Must be healthy and of required age — 1 year for small animals, 2 years for cows.

Selection
3
Make the Niyyah

Make sincere intention for Qurbani for Allah's sake. Face the animal toward Qiblah and say Bismillah Allahu Akbar.

Intention
4
Distribute the Meat

Divide into three equal parts: one-third for family, one-third for relatives and neighbors, one-third for the poor.

Distribution
5
Time Period

Valid from after Eid prayer on 10th Dhul Hijjah until sunset on 12th Dhul Hijjah (3 days total).

Timing
